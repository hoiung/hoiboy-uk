Where fewer than 5 posts share a tag, the remaining slots are topped up by shared category and then by plain recency, so every post renders exactly 5 links. Called from _default/single.html. (blog-priv#66) WHY NOT Hugo's native .Related: it computes ONE combined weighted score against ONE global threshold, so it cannot express a tags-then-category-then- recency fallback CHAIN. Reproduced on the pinned 0.160.0 binary: with tags weight 80, categories weight 10 and threshold 20, a category-only match returns nothing; at threshold 0 it returns. There is no per-call threshold override. Template glue is needed either way, and performance does not break the tie: at 80 posts the hand-rolled intersect costs nothing measurable. Swap this partial for a recency-only stub and time 5 warm `hugo --gc --minify -e production --quiet` runs each way - stub 0.35-0.61s against this partial's 0.48-0.65s, distributions that overlap, so the cost sits under run-to-run variance rather than at any figure worth quoting.
